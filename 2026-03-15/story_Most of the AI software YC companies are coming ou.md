## Summary
<p>As many of you here, I always anticipate and try to see what's coming out from YC companies. I follow https://www.linkedin.com/school/y-combinator/posts/ to see the latest and greatest.<p>Except what I'm finding is that I am constantly being disappointed and frustrated. The software doesn't deliver the promise. And sometimes I wonder how these companies raised money. Some real examples below in just the last week, without mentioning their names:<p>1.  UGC video creator: Workflows I don't understand, and errors after submitting a video for them to clone. 
2. Browser automation: Again, unnatural workflows, constant errors, and their support team keeps replying with "Oh you just have to use that other page and copy the ID from X with Y and we will get back to you about that other bug"
3. QA automation: Sent me a link to get setup. Didn't work - immediate errors. 
4. AI Identity startup: Constant errors in their playground.<p>What YC companies in the AI space have you used in the last months that have truly wow-ed you?</p>
